## Notes on collection

- **IPC Somalia analyses were retrieved via fsnau.org, not ipcinfo.org.** ipcinfo.org returned HTTP 403 (blocked) on every request attempted during collection, including with a browser user agent, so its documents could not be downloaded directly. FSNAU is the body that conducts Somalia's IPC technical analysis (under the IPC Somalia Technical Working Group, convened by FAO/FSNAU with WFP and partners) and hosts the same official IPC products on its own site, so these are the same documents, sourced from the priority-2 domain instead of priority-1 because priority-1 was unreachable.
- **fscluster.org was also tried** as an alternate IPC mirror and returned HTTP 403 (Forbidden) — not used.
- **FEWS NET was excluded per instructions.**
- **Date discrepancy flagged:** `ipc_somalia_post_deyr_2025_findings_feb2026.pdf`'s title slide reads "24 February 2025," while FSNAU's own filename and publications listing date it 24 February 2026 under the "2025 Post Deyr" cycle (the Post Deyr season runs October–December, with results typically published the following February — so a "2025 Post Deyr" analysis published in February 2026 is the expected cadence, but the slide date was not corrected to match). Treat the slide-level date as potentially a template leftover; the file is used here as the 2025/26 Post Deyr cycle briefing.
- All 8 documents are real, were downloaded directly from the organisations' own domains (`fsnau.org`, `docs.wfp.org`), and none of their content was invented, paraphrased, or substituted.
